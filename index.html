YOUR FIRST LAUNCH

Install Luce on your Mac.

Three steps, then you’re ready to open your projects.

  1. Luce Installer
    Luce.app
    →
    Applications
    01

    Drag Luce into Applications.

    Open the downloaded DMG, then drag Luce.app onto the Applications folder. Eject the installer when the copy finishes.

  2. Luce wasn’t opened

    This beta hasn’t been notarized by Apple.

    Done
    02

    Try opening Luce once.

    Open Luce from Applications. macOS may block its first launch because this beta is unnotarized. Dismiss the alert without moving the app to Trash.

  3. System Settings
    Privacy & SecuritySecurity

    Luce was blocked from opening.

    Open Anyway ↖
    03

    Choose “Open Anyway.”

    Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to Security, and select Open Anyway for Luce. Authenticate if asked, then confirm Open.

Can’t see Open Anyway? Try opening Luce from Applications again, then return to Privacy & Security. The option is available for about an hour after a blocked launch. These are illustrations; wording can vary by macOS version. Only approve the Luce copy you downloaded here. Apple’s instructions ↗

Before you start: Apple Silicon Macs (M1 or later) running macOS 13 or newer; Intel Macs are not supported by this download. Install and sign in to Codex or Claude Code separately to use those agents. Phone access requires your own relay setup. Release notes & checksum ↗
